## Voyce
- **OPI** = Over the phone interpretation.
- **VRI** = Video Remote Interpretation 
- **IR** = Interaction Record
    - Unique value registered in salesforce for every call made from a client to a agent. 
    - IR captures details from the moment an agent answers until the call concludes
    - Example: “IR-100004010791”
- **Voyce VIP** = Voyce Intelligent Platform
    - portal used by customers and internal employees to analytics.
